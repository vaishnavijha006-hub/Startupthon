🚀 CodezY – Startup Idea Pitching Event

💡 About the Project

Everyone has ideas, but not everyone gets the chance to turn them into something real.

With this thought, we’re organizing a Startup Idea Pitching Event under CodezY, with permission from our faculty. The aim is to give students a platform where they can share their ideas, showcase their creativity, and take their first step toward building something meaningful.

Through our website, students will be able to register and submit their startup ideas. We’ll review the submissions, shortlist promising ideas, and invite selected students or teams to present their ideas before a panel.

✨ What Can You Do on the Website?

- Register for the event and submit your startup idea.
- Share your idea with a title and description explaining the problem and your proposed solution.
- Choose a domain such as AI, healthcare, finance, education, environment, or any other relevant field.
- Tell us about your prototype — whether it's just an idea, currently in development, or already working.
- Upload a sketch (optional) to help explain your concept visually.

🔄 How Will It Work?

1. Students register through the website.
2. They submit their startup ideas with the required details.
3. Our team reviews the submissions and shortlists promising ideas.
4. Selected students or teams get the opportunity to pitch their ideas before a panel.
5. Pitching sessions may take place over 3–4 days during faculty-approved time slots.

The presentation schedule will be shared manually through the official WhatsApp group.

🌱 What Happens After Shortlisting?

We want to help good ideas go beyond just being ideas. Depending on the quality of the proposal and the resources available, selected participants may get opportunities for:

- Mentorship and guidance
- Funding support
- Help with prototype development
- Technical support and networking

These opportunities will depend on availability and are not guaranteed.

🎯 Why Are We Doing This?

Our goal is simple: to encourage students to think beyond the classroom, solve real-world problems, and explore entrepreneurship.

You don't necessarily need a fully developed product to participate. If you have an interesting idea, a clear understanding of the problem, and a possible solution, this is your chance to share it.

🛠️ Tech Stack

The technologies used to build this platform will be updated as development progresses.

Potential technologies include:

- Frontend: React.js, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Node.js and Express.js
- Database: MongoDB
- Version Control: Git and GitHub

👥 Who Can Participate?

Students who are interested in startups, technology, innovation, and problem-solving are encouraged to participate. Individual and team participation will follow the event guidelines.
